Which subjects do you offer? +

Pure Science tuition is available in Chemistry and Biology. Science Double Award includes Biology, Chemistry and Physics.

How often are the classes? +

Chemistry and Biology each have two 1.5-hour sessions per week. Science Double Award has three 1.5-hour sessions per week.

What is included in Double Science Award? +

Our Double Science Award tuition covers 24 key topics across Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Browse the full topic lists above or contact us for details about classes.

Are classes online or in person? +

Classes are designed to be 100% online, with interactive online tuition.

When does enrollment begin? +

Registration for the January 2027 intake opens on 1 December 2026. You may enquire now using WhatsApp.

How much will tuition cost? +

Chemistry and Biology each cost P250 per month, with 2 sessions per week (1.5 hours each). Science Double Award costs P550 per month, with 3 sessions per week (1.5 hours each).
